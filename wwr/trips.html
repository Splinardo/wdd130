Choose Your Adventure

Kid Adventures

Perfect for families and young kids looking to experience water fun together. This calm yet exciting run features gentle rapids and playful water splashes, ensuring everyone enjoys themselves safely under the care of our expert instructors.

Crazy Rapids

Ready to push your limits? Brave our high-adrenaline white water channels. Face aggressive waves, dramatic vertical drops, and continuous churning currents built explicitly for true adventure seekers looking for a wild challenge.

Half Day Adventure

An action-packed half day of activities designed to deliver maximum excitement in a compressed timeframe. Perfect for groups and visitors looking to test their paddling skills and enjoy premium rapids without committing to a full day layout.
